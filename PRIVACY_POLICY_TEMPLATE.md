# Privacy policy — starter template

Not needed while you're the only user (`DISABLE_REGISTRATION: 'true'`). Have
something like this live on the site before flipping registration open to
others. This is a starting draft, not legal advice — worth a quick review
by someone qualified once you're ready to publish it, especially if users
outside Singapore start signing up.

---

**What we collect**
- Your email and login details, to run your account
- The social media accounts you connect, and a secure access token for each
  one (this is what lets us post on your behalf — we do not see or store
  your platform passwords, only a token the platform itself issues us)
- The posts and schedules you create

**What we don't do**
- We don't sell or share your data with advertisers or third parties
- We don't read your drafts except when necessary to fix a technical
  problem you've reported

**How to leave**
- You can disconnect any connected account at any time
- You can request full deletion of your account and data by contacting
  [your contact email]

**Where data is stored**
- On a server operated by Samyag Synergy, in [your server's region]

**Changes**
- If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll let you know before
  it takes effect
